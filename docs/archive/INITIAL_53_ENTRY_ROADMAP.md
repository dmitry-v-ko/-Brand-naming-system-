## Editorial acceptance criteria

A planned method becomes `ready` only once it has a complete `SKILL.md` following [`SKILL_SPEC.md`](../SKILL_SPEC.md), a realistic research procedure with source-verification behavior, failure handling, a semi-developed preview step, and a synthetic labeled example.

Reject duplicates whose only difference is the sector searched, era, or language. Document those as **lexical universes** or parameters instead.
