# Cycle 01 independent critique

Review date: 2026-11-10. Requirements authority: `docs/context.md`. This review inspected all 18 existing Markdown files, including all five skill files, the research report and its addendum, both templates, the conductor, and the demonstration. It evaluates the repository as delivered, not the quality an unusually capable agent might supply from its own prior knowledge.

## Score: 4.0 / 10

The repository is a useful and unusually honest foundation, but is not yet an independently usable, developed naming-method library. Its best material defines how a session ought to behave. Its weakest material is the actual creative instruction: all five methods remain drafts, have very short illustrative outputs rather than worked runs, and have no recorded execution evidence. The substantial research report is useful input, but has not yet become operational skills.

### Fixed rubric

Use these same weights and anchors in later cycles. A 9 means a practically usable library with deep narrow skills, appropriate breadth, recoverable state, usable research routes, and demonstrated agent scenarios. It does not require consumer research, commercial success, software, clearance, or a numerical minimum of methods.

## Strengths to preserve

- `AGENTS.md` and `docs/SESSION_PROTOCOL.md` make the user a creative navigator, permit delegation and direct state jumps, preserve parked branches, and forbid automatic finalization.
- `docs/context.md` explicitly prioritizes detailed narrow techniques and removes the numerical quota.
- `docs/research/BRANDING_METHODS_RESEARCH.md` distinguishes source support from editorial procedures, records access limitations, and resists pretending that name categories or source domains are algorithms. The addendum correctly preserves narrow sound and construction techniques.
- Source status, shortlist consent, outside-repository session storage, and the exclusion of clearance are clearly stated.
- `lexical-universes/UNIVERSES.md` offers a promising breadth of surprising worlds and correctly treats them as parameters rather than separate methods.

## Blocking defects and acceptance checks

### P0 — Skills do not yet carry their own operational depth

**Paths:** all five `skills/*/SKILL.md`; `docs/SKILL_SPEC.md`.

Every skill repeats the same general use/avoid paragraph and nearly identical failure warnings. The word-blending example is two names; phonetic invention is three names; cross-language mining explicitly avoids giving any real root; the fiction skill supplies one invented result. None demonstrates the intermediate search, a weak choice, the decision that repairs it, feedback, and a traceable final batch. Asking an agent to collect 25–40 terms or produce 30 forms does not tell it how to select or diversify them.

**Acceptance checks:**

1. Each skill offered as ready has specific use/avoid conditions, defaults, intermediate artifacts, transformation rules, diversification rules, and stopping/recovery choices. A reader can execute it without inventing its central operator.
2. Each has a complete small worked run: brief, contrasting previews, selected direction, ingredient/research ledger, transformation decisions, contrasting candidates with pronunciation where useful, rejected weak result, repair, user feedback, and the resulting state change. Synthetic dialogue is acceptable when clearly labeled; researched vocabulary must have real support.
3. Construction skills show exact retained/deleted/overlapping segments. Sound skills show actual phoneme or syllable constraints, stress, contrasting palettes, and reasons to discard awkward forms. Cross-language work shows exact dictionary senses and adaptations. Metaphor work shows both relevant and misleading transferred properties. Literary-pattern work shows the observed pattern before applying it to new material.
4. Readiness is supported by review evidence rather than inferred from the presence of headings.

### P0 — Narrow-technique requirement is acknowledged but not implemented

**Paths:** `skills/word-blending/SKILL.md`; `skills/phonetic-invention/SKILL.md`; `docs/METHOD_CATALOG.md`; `docs/research/BRANDING_METHODS_RESEARCH.md`.

The blending skill combines head-tail assembly, genuine overlap, and compressed syllable blending. The catalog still lists umbrella `affix-reframing`, while research recommends separate prefix and suffix techniques. Many literary/scientific catalog rows describe sources rather than operators. The research report diagnoses these problems, but the navigable collection has not absorbed the findings. Catalog family headings alone do not satisfy the requirement to document narrow operations in depth.

**Acceptance checks:**

1. Audit active entries by inputs, primary operator, intermediate artifact, and output. Record the closest neighbor and the distinguishing decision, without requiring an arbitrary count.
2. Document useful narrow techniques separately; use umbrella pages and families for navigation. The owner's additional clarification relayed during review explicitly names compounding, fragment blending, overlap telescoping, clipping, affixation, syllable recombination, and phonetic invention for individual treatment. Preserve these distinct operations; affixation can have separate prefix and suffix procedures where useful. This is requested coverage, not an arbitrary method quota.
3. Replace the historical roadmap as the primary operational inventory; preserve its old IDs in a migration record. Reclassify corpus-only entries as universes or clearly justified research procedures, preserving useful source routes. Do not erase narrow phonetic operators merely because they share a family.
4. Route representative briefs to genuinely different operators: a direct semantic name, structural invention, a sound-led form, and externally researched material. Demonstrate differences in the operations and resulting candidate clusters, not just in source topics or endings. This is a coverage check, not a mandatory four-skill quota.

### P0 — No demonstrated usable outcomes or session scenarios

**Paths:** `examples/DEMO.md`; `docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`; absence of scenario records in `examples/`.

The demo announces research but never performs it or displays a candidate. Its method menu mostly mixes operators and source worlds; despite claiming examples are illustrative, it gives no actual example words. The final user asks to save an unspecified candidate that was never shown, and the agent claims to add “that exact candidate.” This cannot demonstrate correct consent or exact-spelling persistence. There is no evidence of a completed independent run, recovery, or resume.

**Acceptance checks:**

1. Replace or supplement the demo with an actual small walkthrough that displays previews, cited source terms when applicable, candidates, exact selection, and matching before/after state. Label simulated user feedback honestly.
2. Run and record fresh-context agent walkthroughs using only the repository and ordinary research tools. Include at least: research with a dead source; sound/construction refinement; branch switch and resume; a delegated direct instruction; and export of explicit favorites only. Scenarios can share one integrated transcript if coverage is clear.
3. Inspect the actual results: distinct candidate clusters, accurate transformations, recoverable parked paths, no invented citations, no implicit saves, English names during non-English conversation, and no automatic finish.
4. Store only synthetic review artifacts in the library; keep any real session files outside it. Record shortcomings and fixes, not merely a blanket pass declaration. No consumer study or software test harness is required.

### P1 — Templates and output contracts lose state required by the protocol

**Paths:** `templates/EXPLORATION_LOG.md:19`; `templates/SHORTLIST.md:10`; `docs/SESSION_PROTOCOL.md`; all five skill output contracts; `docs/SOURCES_AND_RESEARCH.md`.

The exploration template lacks explicit branch parent, stable candidate IDs, source IDs, and candidate-pool summary. The shortlist template has no separate evidence status or candidate ID. Skills still offer combined statuses such as “verified attested,” “adapted,” and “invented,” whereas the protocol correctly separates candidate form from evidence. An adapted candidate therefore has no consistent place for the verification status of its source. A sufficiently careful agent can extend the templates, but the supplied defaults encourage incomplete records.

**Acceptance checks:**

1. Align templates and skill outputs around stable branch/candidate/source IDs and separate form/evidence fields. Preserve source term, supported sense, transformation, and uncertainty without requiring readers to reconstruct them from prose.
2. Provide a compact populated synthetic example, including a parked parent branch, a hybrid with ordered methods, one verified source adapted into an invented form, one unverified lead, and repeated selection without duplication.
3. Resume from those files in fresh context and recover exact favorites, rejected directions, active state, pending opportunities, and sources without invented prior decisions. Verify that an export contains every protocol-required field.

### P1 — Research is credible background, but not yet a usable lexical trail

**Paths:** `docs/SOURCES_AND_RESEARCH.md`; `docs/research/BRANDING_METHODS_RESEARCH.md`; research sections of the five skills; `lexical-universes/UNIVERSES.md`.

The research report contains meaningful attributed method research and is not a source-free document. However, it explicitly says dictionary/corpus operational access was not validated. Skills still say “reliable dictionaries” or “historical SF dictionaries” without demonstrating a concrete retrieval and extraction path. Universe rows provide source classes and mood labels, but no actual preview words, verified starter entries, or practiced fallbacks. Consequently the agent still supplies most of the difficult research work from its own initiative.

**Acceptance checks:**

1. For every ready research-dependent skill, demonstrate one actual accessible source path with exact consulted entry/work, supported term or pattern, meaning, transformation, and candidate. Link the skill to relevant research evidence; label editorial technique details separately.
2. Demonstrate one unavailable or insufficient source and the resulting alternative research route or explicit unverified branch. Do not require a particular browser vendor or paid resource.
3. Enrich representative universe cards with evocative labeled previews, concrete research entry points and queries, enabled operators, and predictable dead ends. The menu should be useful even when the user supplies no source world.
4. Keep invented candidates distinct from attested ingredients. Do not use historical brand success stories or simulated impressions as proof of effectiveness.

### P2 — Maintenance guidance and distribution remain incomplete

**Paths:** `docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`; `README.md`; `docs/METHOD_CATALOG.md`.

The plan says published-source research “has not yet been performed,” despite a substantial dated research report now existing. The catalog remains a 53-entry roadmap while its research proposes a materially revised structure. Quick start is generic rather than showing a concrete Codex Desktop setup and session-directory example. These issues are less serious than weak skills but leave maintainers and users with inconsistent navigation.

**Acceptance checks:** update milestones to distinguish completed method research from unperformed skill trials; make catalog counts and labels reflect the actual files; give a reproducible local/Codex Desktop start and outside-checkout session path; validate local links. Keep licensing and remote publication honestly unresolved until the owner decides. Neither a license decision nor remote publication is necessary to improve or validate the naming library locally.

## Recommended order

1. Align state fields and replace the broken demonstration so later runs produce inspectable evidence.
2. Develop a small contrasting batch of narrow skills to the existing acceptance standard; resolve umbrella boundaries during that work.
3. Connect those skills to real lexical sources and richer universe cards, then run fresh-context scenarios and repair observed failures.
4. Reconcile the roadmap and distribution instructions with the accepted files. Expand only where an additional operator creates a useful creative route.

A high score should follow demonstrated quality of this loop, not the size of the catalog or the polish of plans. The current repository provides good intentions and governance, but most operational capability remains to be built and shown.
