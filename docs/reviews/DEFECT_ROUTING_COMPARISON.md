# Equal-budget refinement comparison

Date: 2026-11-11. Old procedure: commit `d5662c8`. New procedure: frozen pre-review working snapshot, with source hashes retained privately. A real owner-supplied brief was held constant; private names, sources, raw tool results and session histories remain outside this library. One fresh-context generator per arm used the same inherited model and investigated the same two source worlds. This is a comparison of two procedures, not a demonstration of one worker per universe.

## Frozen design and actual resource use

Both arms received the same brief, two initial source branches, source-recognition assumption, eight selectable-name ceiling, two standing creative controls and product contexts. Neither could read the other arm or prior detailed trial outputs. Isolation was instructed, not filesystem-enforced. New sound controls consumed the same eight-name ceiling. Candidate pools and creative rankings were frozen before the market exercise; neither regenerated after findings.

| Resource | Same allowance per arm | Old actual | New actual |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Lexical search query items | 4 | 2 | 2 |
| Lexical retrieval attempts | 6 | 2 | 3 |
| Market search query items | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| Market retrieval attempts | 4 | 3 | 4 |
| Selectable generated names, including anchors/controls | 8 | 8 | 6 |

Each query in a batched call and each open/click/find attempt counts separately, including failures/retries. Requests and full tool responses were logged per event; a local audit recomputed counts, candidate ceilings and clipping maps. No allowance transferred between phases. These are equal maximum search budgets, not equal actual page use, tokens, time or billing. Both used six search queries overall; old used five retrievals and new seven. No additional hidden candidate pool was reported; this is not a runtime sandbox proof.

A review clarification delivered to both generators explained that a replacement occupies its original branch and the same budget; neither used replacement, so that route was not live-tested. Frozen inputs were preserved with an addendum. Market comparison used the same post-generation exercise in both arms: it evaluates findings and compliance, not the causal effect of moving the market gate earlier.

## Observed outputs

Old produced two direct anchors and six clipping variants. New produced two direct anchors, two clipping variants and two independent phonetic controls; it kept an already short direct word rather than shortening it by default. Its object/action/outcome ingredient bank was recorded, with no forced grammatical quota. Neither generator established a clear improvement over standing alternatives.

The two arm pools contained fourteen records and twelve distinct spellings. Two prior controls made fourteen exact spellings for the evaluator. A fresh reader received shuffled, deduplicated spellings, the shared brief and identical contexts only: no arm labels, source stories, generator ranks, market evidence or prior-control labels. It saved form-only observations before contextual assessment. Its four testing choices were two prior controls and two sound-only controls from the new arm; no research-derived candidate from either arm entered that set. The prior controls and one regenerated form also had session history: reappearance is not novelty or clearance.

Market lookup withheld the new arm's strongest sound lead after verifying close financial product uses on primary pages. The other inspected new lead retained an unresolved neighboring financial use. Old lookup found other-category software use and personal-AI neighbors. These observations are separate from creative ratings and registry screening. No final name or user favorite was selected. The outcome supports keeping an independent sound comparison and visible market limits; it does not establish generally better names from the new procedure.

## Problems and repairs

1. A market look requiring two or three leads conflicted with zero/one valid winner. The final rule accepts one lead and never requires padding.
2. Replacement counting was ambiguous. It now stays in its initial branch, counts any selectable new form against the same candidate ceiling, and cannot start a chain.
3. The phonetic child procedure's default eight spoken forms/six spellings conflicted with a two-control allowance. Both the conductor and child now explicitly cap the generated spoken and written pool for this use.
4. The new baseline combined form/context/story in one pre-market artifact. Separate pre-story freezing was not demonstrated; it remains a baseline limitation. The final rule requires a separate checkpoint when file tools exist and prohibits retroactive proof by rearranging a report.
5. Lexical lens order and the exploration template were aligned with their owning rules.

A separate targeted diagnostic under the final rules created exactly two sound forms, with no web actions or additions to the baseline pool. Its plan, form/context checkpoint and result were saved in order; baseline hashes remained unchanged. Independent review also manually exercised one-lead handling, replacement accounting, the two-control override and refusal to retroactively claim stage order. These are scoped checks, not a whole paired rerun or automatic enforcement.

## Limits and interpretation

This is one paired live trial and one editorial evaluator, not an audience experiment or a statistically reliable ranking. Source selection, stochastic generation and unequal actual page use remain confounds; multiple simultaneous changes prevent attributing outcomes to any one change. The modified procedure did not beat the prior controls decisively. Replacement, other adaptation operators, real native-speaker response, actual trademark screening and cross-client execution were not retested. All generation methods remain draft. Public readers cannot independently reproduce the private brief from this aggregate report.
