# Operational method catalog

The active core has **18 separately documented techniques**: six structural, four sound, five source-discovery, one observed-pattern transfer, one semantic and one orthographic. All have complete editorial procedures and synthetic worked traces. Their maturity remains **draft pending per-skill acceptance**; use as labeled trials. A procedure is not accepted merely because headings exist. See [manual validation](reviews/CYCLE_01_MANUAL_VALIDATION.md), [authoring gates](SKILL_SPEC.md), and [source evidence](research/LEXICAL_ACCESS_EVIDENCE.md). No method-count quota applies.

## Route a brief by the operation

| Brief need | Suggested trial | Illustrative outcome / actual difference |
|---|---|---|
| Clear, welcoming workshop access | intact-word-compounding | Open Bench; both familiar words remain intact |
| Compact semantic invention | fragment-blending or overlap-telescoping | Weavora loses source segments; Gleamber shares am once |
| Precise abstract product | phonetic-invention | Telo; constructed sound slots, no etymology |
| Fleeting drafts, evocative real term | lexical-research | Virga; unchanged verified NWS ingredient, adverse implication retained |
| Reliable handoff with imagery | metaphor-mining | Next Hand; relation transfer plus declared compounding, evaporation rejected |
| Momentum with a foreign sense | cross-language-mining | Elan Step; verified French sense + explicit accent removal and compound |
| An unfamiliar evening word | historical-lexical-archaeology | Gloaming; dated regional sense recovery, no mandatory spelling change |
| A documented origin story about guidance | etymological-archaeology | Clew; historical nodes separated from speculative ancestry |
| An observed writerly construction | literary-word-formation | Flexidy; inspected pattern followed by unrelated fragment inputs |
| Ingredient discovery from contrasting texts | corpus-contrast | Horizon; measured sample context, not a global rarity claim |
| A name echoing a product-use sound | onomatopoeic-naming | Declared event features become a sound-to-spelling map; no invented listening claim |
| A repeated rhythmic signature | reduplication | NekNak; base plus controlled vowel echo |
| A fixed spoken name with new spelling | respelling | Kove; supplied cove target, c→k with reading risk |

These are fixture illustrations, not recommended brands or a validated market shortlist. Full traces live in each skill.

## Owner scope decision

Double meaning was removed as a standalone method on 2026-11-10. Its [former procedure](archive/RETIRED_DOUBLE_MEANING.md) is archived for historical review references only. Multiple meanings may remain candidate notes during lexical research, without a separate method/menu/branch. Verified rare words can be used unchanged; no construction step is mandatory. Earlier reviews describe the then-current twelve-procedure library, not the current count.
