## Repetition route

Search the official domain plus the exact headword; open the result; find the headword in the page; inspect the surrounding definition rather than navigation. If redirected, follow one documented link or switch to a second reference. Retain failure records. If no source exposes the sense, preserve the lead as unverified and stop semantic claims. Dictionaries verify lexical senses, not the quality of a name.
