# Research basis for conductor improvements

Reviewed 2026-11-10. These are selective paraphrases of public practitioner guidance; agency descriptions and cases do not independently establish effectiveness. The execution contract is our editorial adaptation, not a method attributed wholesale to an accelerator.

| Source | Supported practice | Integration and limit |
|---|---|---|
| [Techstars: Position Your Product](https://toolkit.techstars.com/position-your-product) | Audience, problem, value, alternatives and differentiation | Concise shared brief; positioning, not a name-generation operator |
| [Techstars: Brand Your Business](https://toolkit.techstars.com/brand-your-business-worksheet) | Name, short category descriptor, readability, pronunciation and customer reactions | Separate descriptor and observed feedback; its availability checks remain outside this library |
| [YC / Michael Seibel: How to Pitch Your Company](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/how-to-pitch-your-company/) | Explain what the company does plainly | Test message comprehension; this does not establish a YC naming algorithm |
| [Lexicon: Brand Naming Process](https://www.lexiconbranding.com/brand-naming-process/) | Strategic framing and multiple creative perspectives | Contrasting directions; one worker per library method is our design |
| [Zinzin: Competitive Namescape](https://www.zinzin.com/process/competitive-namescape/) | Map competitive naming conventions | Bounded sourced sample; no uniqueness or clearance claim |
| [Igor: Naming Process](https://www.igorinternational.com/process/) | Competitive analysis, positioning and generation | Support for framing; legal stages excluded |
| [Catchword: Linguistics and Cultural Analysis](https://catchwordbranding.com/naming/linguistics-cultural-analysis/) | Native-speaker checks for pronunciation and associations | Recommend actual country-specific review; no simulated evidence |
| [Zinzin: Linguistic Screening](https://www.zinzin.com/process/linguistic-screening/) | Meaning, slang, connotations and pronunciation | Keep unperformed review unknown |

[Paul Graham's Change Your Name](https://paulgraham.com/name.html) is a personal essay, not official YC policy. Its exact .com advice concerns US startups and is not adopted as a universal rule. This update does not implement trademark/domain clearance, assign numeric consumer-effectiveness scores or increase the eighteen-method count.
