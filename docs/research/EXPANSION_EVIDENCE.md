# Seven-method expansion: evidence index

Date: 2026-11-10. The owner approved seven detailed additions, bringing the active catalog from eleven to eighteen. All seven remain draft pending independent acceptance. This index points to actual research records, not a claim that every source universe or every procedure has been independently tested.

| Methods | Consulted evidence | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Historical lexical archaeology, etymological archaeology, literary word formation | [Historical and literary evidence](HISTORICAL_METHODS_EVIDENCE.md) | Dated usage does not prove obsolescence; reconstructed forms remain marked; a textual occurrence does not prove authorial invention or intent. |
| Corpus contrast | [Bounded corpus comparison](CORPUS_CONTRAST_EVIDENCE.md) | Two specialist topical samples, not a general-English rarity measure. Sources, spans, token rules, counts and denominators are explicit. |
| Onomatopoeic naming, reduplication, respelling | [Sound and orthography evidence](SOUND_ORTHOGRAPHY_EVIDENCE.md) | Imagined events are not listened-to audio; echo units are controlled constructions; frozen pronunciation is a target, not a measured audience reading. |

Sources support only their identified facts. Naming procedures, branching rules, matrices, synthetic briefs and candidate judgments are editorial adaptations. Direct use of verified rare words remains valid; transformations are optional. Legal/commercial availability and consumer response are outside this evidence.

Previous critiques describe earlier inventories. The eighteen-method collection completed its own independent critique and improvement cycle under the existing rubric, reaching [9.11/10](../reviews/CYCLE_05_CRITIQUE.md) within a bounded documented scope; this does not promote every method to ready. The retired double-meaning procedure remains historical material and is not reintroduced by literary paired-attribute compression or any other addition.
